% Discussion paragraph for the forecasting experiment (Table 2, v3, final). 2026-10-05.
Several caveats bound the interpretation of our forecasting results. First, the official
monthly price series runs only 16 consecutive observations (2025-04 to 2026-07) and the
weekly series 52 weeks, of which 5 are linearly interpolated (3 falling inside the
evaluation window; excluding them leaves all model rankings and headline metrics
essentially unchanged). Rolling-origin evaluation therefore yields only 6 monthly and
26 weekly origins, and the event-window subset contains merely 3 monthly folds, so
moderate differences between models are not statistically distinguishable. Second, our
event features cover only the 60 seed documents of the gold set plus six manually
verified key policies; they are an incomplete and possibly misaligned proxy of the true
policy-event stream. We thus read the event-augmentation outcomes as conservative,
descriptive evidence under incomplete event coverage, not as a lower bound on the true
effect: missing events may attenuate the estimated gain, while sparse noisy indicators
may also contaminate it. Third, we disclose two settings for event features: an oracle
setting with same-period features (an upper-bound, calendar-known scenario) and a
real-time lag-1 setting. Event features improve MAE in only a small minority of the 16
ablation cells (2/16 oracle, 1/16 lag-1). The only systematic pattern we observe is
that, for most models on the weekly series (3 of 4 families), lag-1 features degrade
accuracy by less than oracle features; this pattern does not hold overall (7/16 cells)
and the average degradation is in fact larger under lag-1 (0.286 vs. 0.188 yuan), so we
do not generalize it. All event-augmented variants produce non-flat forecasts, whereas
some baselines (ARIMA in particular) still degenerate to flat forecasts on the monthly
series; correspondingly, tie-aware direction accuracy improves from undefined for such
flat baselines to 67-83% in selected oracle settings. Three cells show numerical MAE
gains of 0.01-0.05 yuan, but paired bootstrap and Diebold-Mariano tests (n=6 folds)
yield p-values of 0.78-0.96; they constitute no stable or statistically meaningful
evidence. Overall, short-term green-certificate prices exhibit strong persistence, and
policy features do not yet yield stable incremental predictive power at the current
data density. A systematic evaluation with richer event coverage, longer official
series, and Diebold-Mariano tests across all cells is left to future work.
